# List 07: Finance and Accounting 財務與會計

200 TOEIC words about money, banking, budgets, and insurance, chosen from the TOEIC Service List (TSL) and Business Service List (BSL). Each word has a picture, a Chinese meaning, a **simple sentence**, and a **TOEIC sentence**.
200 個 TOEIC 單字，選自 TSL 與 BSL 字表。每個字都有圖示、中文意思、一句簡單例句，以及一句 TOEIC 風格的例句。

Study 25 words a day, so this list takes eight days. 每天學 25 個字，八天學完。

**Key 詞性:** (n) noun 名詞 · (v) verb 動詞 · (adj) adjective 形容詞 · (adv) adverb 副詞
