# List 09: Manufacturing 製造

200 TOEIC words about factories, equipment, and technology, chosen from the TOEIC Service List (TSL) and Business Service List (BSL). Each word has a picture, a Chinese meaning, a **simple sentence**, and a **TOEIC sentence**.

Study 25 words a day, so this list takes eight days. 每天學 25 個字，八天學完。

**Key 詞性:** (n) noun 名詞 · (v) verb 動詞 · (adj) adjective 形容詞 · (adv) adverb 副詞

- **Simple:** Set the parameter to ten. 把參數設為十。
- **TOEIC:** The project must stay within the budget parameters. 專案必須維持在預算範圍內。
- **Related 相關字 / easily confused 易混淆:** perimeter 周邊（易混淆）; limit 限制（近義）

### 197. 🌀 distortion (n) 扭曲；失真

- **Meaning:** a change that makes something false or unclear
- **Simple:** The mirror causes distortion. 這面鏡子會讓影像失真。
- **TOEIC:** The audio system produces no distortion at high volume. 這套音響在大音量下不會失真。
- **Word family 字族 / phrases:** distort (v)
- **Related 相關字 / easily confused 易混淆:** deformation 變形（近義）; distraction 分心（易混淆）

### 198. 🖨️ replicate (v) 複製；重現

- **Meaning:** to copy or repeat exactly
- **Simple:** Can you replicate this picture? 你能複製這張圖嗎？
- **TOEIC:** Other firms cannot easily replicate our production method. 其他公司很難複製我們的生產方法。
- **Word family 字族 / phrases:** replica (n), replication (n)
- **Related 相關字 / easily confused 易混淆:** copy 複製（近義）; duplicate 複製（近義）

### 199. 🎯 optimum (adj) 最佳的

- **Meaning:** best possible for a situation
- **Simple:** This is the optimum time to go. 這是出發的最佳時間。
- **TOEIC:** Keep the machine at its optimum temperature for best results. 請將機器維持在最佳溫度以獲得最好的結果。
- **Word family 字族 / phrases:** optimize (v), optimal (adj)
- **Related 相關字 / easily confused 易混淆:** optimal 最理想的（近義）; minimum 最小的（易混淆）

### 200. 🔧 tighten (v) 收緊；加強

- **Meaning:** to make something firmer or stricter
- **Simple:** Please tighten the screw. 請把螺絲鎖緊。
- **TOEIC:** The company will tighten its security after the data leak. 資料外洩後，公司將加強安全措施。
- **Word family 字族 / phrases:** tight (adj), tightly (adv)
- **Related 相關字 / easily confused 易混淆:** loosen 放鬆（反義）; tight 緊的（字族）
