# List 01: Office and Daily Work 辦公室與日常工作

200 TOEIC words about the office and everyday work, chosen from the TOEIC Service List (TSL) and Business Service List (BSL). Each word has a picture, a Chinese meaning, a **simple sentence**, and a **TOEIC sentence**.
200 個 TOEIC 單字，選自 TSL 與 BSL 字表。每個字都有圖示、中文意思、一句簡單例句，以及一句 TOEIC 風格的例句。

Study 25 words a day, so this list takes eight days. 每天學 25 個字，八天學完。

**Key 詞性:** (n) noun 名詞 · (v) verb 動詞 · (adj) adjective 形容詞 · (adv) adverb 副詞

- **Simple:** I presume you are tired. 我想你累了。
- **TOEIC:** We presume that all invoices have been paid by now. 我們假定所有發票到現在都已付清。
- **Word family 字族 / phrases:** presumption (n), presumably (adv)
- **Related 相關字 / easily confused 易混淆:** assume 假設（近義）; resume 恢復；履歷（易混淆）
