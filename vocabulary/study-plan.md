# TOEIC 700: Vocabulary Study Plan 單字學習計畫

> **中文摘要：** 這份計畫適合目前約 450–600 分、目標 700 分的學習者。每天 30–40 分鐘，每週 5–6 天，共 10 週，約學 2000 個 TOEIC 核心單字。
> 每天流程：複習 10 分鐘 → 學新字 15 分鐘（先讀簡單例句，再讀 TOEIC 例句）→ 聽發音 5 分鐘 → 小測驗 5 分鐘。
> 重點：用片語記單字（如 *meet a deadline*）、記整個字族（*apply / applicant / application*）、注意易混淆字（*personal / personnel*）、依第 1、3、7、14、30 天間隔複習。

**Who this is for:** learners scoring roughly 450–600 who want to reach about 700.
**Time needed:** 30–40 minutes a day, 5–6 days a week, for 10 weeks.

## Why vocabulary matters for 700

A score of 700 usually means about 350 on Listening and 350 on Reading. At that level, the most common reason for wrong answers is not grammar. It is unknown words in Part 5 (incomplete sentences), Part 7 (reading passages), and Parts 3–4 (conversations and talks).

TOEIC uses a fairly predictable set of business words. The 2000 words in this folder are the TOEIC Service List (TSL, 1,249 words) plus 751 words from the Business Service List (BSL), both by Browne & Culligan (CC BY-SA 4.0, newgeneralservicelist.org). They are meant to be learned together with the 2,800 basic words of the NGSL, which most learners at 450–600 already know. If you know about **1,500–2,000 core TOEIC words** well, including how they are used in a sentence, 700 is a realistic goal.

## A daily routine (35 minutes)

1. **Review (10 min).** Go through words from 1, 3, 7, and 14 days ago. Cover the meaning and say it out loud before you check.
2. **Learn new words (15 min).** Study 12–15 new words. For each word:
   - Look at the picture and read the Chinese and English meaning.
   - Read the **simple sentence** first, then the **TOEIC sentence**.
   - Note the word family (noun, verb, adjective).
   - Write **your own** sentence about your job or life.
3. **Listen (5 min).** Say each new word aloud, or look up its pronunciation. TOEIC Listening uses American, British, Canadian, and Australian accents.
4. **Quiz (5 min).** Do the quiz at the end of the list without looking. Mark the words you missed for tomorrow's review.

## Study tips that work for TOEIC

- **Learn words in phrases, not alone.** TOEIC tests collocations. Learn *meet a deadline*, not just *deadline*.
- **Learn the whole word family.** Part 5 often asks you to choose between *competitive, competition, compete, competitively*. Knowing the suffixes (-tion, -ive, -ly, -ment) earns easy points.
- **Watch for similar words.** *Personal / personnel*, *affect / effect*, *lend / borrow*, *rise / raise* are common traps.
- **Use spaced review.** Words you review on days 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 stay in long-term memory. Cramming does not.
- **Read real business English.** Short company emails, notices, and job ads look just like Part 7 passages.
- **Test yourself weekly.** Every Sunday, take 20 random words from all lists and check how many you remember. Aim for 80% or more.

## How to know you are ready

- You know at least 85% of the words in every list in this folder.
- You can finish Part 5 (30 questions) in about 10 minutes with 24 or more correct.
- In Part 7, you rarely stop because of an unknown word.

When all three are true, you are likely in the 700 range. Take a full practice test to confirm.
